Support

How the experience works

  • Find one of the printed plaques on the physical site model.
  • From the landing page, tap "View with AR."
  • On iPhone, Safari will show an App Clip card — tap it to launch the AR experience without installing anything from the App Store.
  • Point your camera at a plaque to lock tracking, then explore the model and tap any glowing hotspot for more information.

Troubleshooting

  • No App Clip card appears: this requires Safari on iOS 16.4 or later. Other browsers and older iOS versions fall back to the same experience running directly in the browser instead.
  • Tracking won't lock: get close to the plaque, make sure it's well lit, and hold the phone steady for a moment.
  • Nothing loads: check your internet connection — the experience downloads 3D content on first use.

Requirements

An iPhone or Android phone with a camera and a modern browser. The native App Clip experience (no install) is available on iPhone with iOS 16.4+; all other supported devices use the in-browser AR experience automatically.
