Ramapo AR

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

Ramapo AR (including its App Clip) is an augmented-reality viewer for a physical site model. This page describes what happens with your data when you use it.

Accounts

There are none. The experience has no sign-in, no user profiles, and no persistent user identifiers.

Camera

The app requests camera access to run the AR experience. Camera frames are processed on your device to track the physical plaque and render the overlay — they are not recorded, stored, or sent to any server.

What is sent over the network

  • The app downloads 3D models, images, and UI assets from our own server (ar-ramapo.onrender.com) so it can render the experience.
  • Card/hotspot text content is fetched from a public, read-only content sheet used to power the informational cards.
  • No form fields, account data, or personal information are collected or transmitted by the app itself.

Third-party components

The experience is built on standard AR/rendering engines (including image-tracking and web AR libraries). As part of normal operation, these components — and our hosting provider — may log routine technical information such as device type, IP address, or performance metrics, the same way any web server or AR engine does. This information is not used to personally identify you and is not sold.

Children

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "last updated" date.
